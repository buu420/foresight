## Behavior

Story Events inside Lost Sanctum follows accepted local errands, then the current
area and era, while retaining independent quest choices. New introductions remain
in People until accepted. Missing progress gives an explicit unavailable note;
it does not redirect a local Sanctum errand to Lavos. Hub ties no longer favor a
600 AD errand over a usable prehistoric errand.

The Lightless Cave warp no longer inherits an unrelated village-ambush guard.
Its narrow opening uses the existing fine-grid native-trigger approach, including
the reported floor levels. The Waystone routes through the stone/pedestal's shared
native Confirm handler. Bridge contacts use the live actor's exact foot row; the
Nu Master and Reptite bridge builder have identifiable People entries. The native
workers continue to block onward travel until bridge completion.

Primeval Fortress545/554 touch-opening passages expose collision-proven goals
behind their live, enabled native terrain copies. The staged route reaches the
real opening contact and waits for fresh native terrain. A preview never supplies
movement authority. Passage letters match their exit letters.

Winding Passage578/581 can use the existing selectable short-step gait for
auto-walk. Manual guidance explains the current native Dash control at a verified
walking-only turn and says to resume the usual speed at the next direction. This
last instruction matters: staying at walking speed indefinitely can itself stall
a later floor crossing. Forced-running auto-walk stops with instructions to tap
Dash to select walking and restart. The mod never synthesizes that tap or changes
the Run setting. Native Confirm, quest choices, locks, battles and timing remain
under the game/player's control.
