The [release notes](v0.3.44.md) give controls and request conveyor/K speech, inspection-drop recovery, both crane directions, the hatch fight/terminal/ladder sequence, and a full bike race followed by exploration. **No completed live Factory route or bike-race listening/playability test was performed during publication.** Those limits remain explicit. Missing speech and failed routes should be reported with the latest Reloaded log. Real combat, Confirm, puzzle input, bike steering, and boosts remain with the player.
