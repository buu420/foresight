Released October 26, 2026 as the next tester beta following the user's approval of the Black Omen elevator Story Events correction and the ongoing public tester-update workflow.

Release: https://github.com/buu420/foresight/releases/tag/v0.3.77

No movement, physics, collision, flag, ride, battle or Confirm behavior was changed.
