## Validation limits

The final full managed suite passed 3,718 tests: Native 1,166, Core 226, Prism 10
and Mod 2,316, with zero failures or skips. All 33 selected packaged contracts
passed after rebuilding from the release tag. Release checks passed four
Python, six AMM manifest and 18 release-script regressions, plus six
deployment-path checks and the canonical-path checks. The ten new raw voice
clips passed local transcription, non-silence, clipping and timing checks.

Fresh read-only Ghidra 12.1.2 exports, installed script bytes and terrain,
rendered Nu frames, the exact original movie, Descript review and independent
source review support the
[implementation evidence](../native-audits/2026-10-15-epoch-nu-and-seals.md).
Review found and resolved repeated-conversation narration suppression before
the final suite. Muted standalone x86 native topology probes passed the exact
Epoch movie/master through natural completion, pause/resume and early close
with zero failed events and continuous sample delivery.

No game was launched. Live traversal, controller/Confirm behavior, managed movie
hook registration in a game session, in-game listening and audible synchronization
still need tester confirmation. Package checks and standalone media probes are
not live gameplay proof. Later accepted-quest puzzle instructions were outside
the bounded introduction audit; whole-game audio-description coverage remains
incomplete.
