# Foresight 0.3.78 tester build

World navigation prepares each story route once per objective instead of repeating the same work for every entrance. Navigation also excludes its own synchronous capture and speech from a connected controller's poll timeout. Actual input pauses, disconnections, scene changes and manual control still stop movement. Unexpected menu closures are spoken and logged with a reason.

World-map entrance routes choose a contact clear of a parked Epoch or Dactyl boarding area. They prefer extra clearance while retaining a usable native boundary when needed. Flight routes choose legal landing sites that leave a walk-connected entrance contact clear. If a parked vehicle covers every usable entrance contact, the target explains that it needs repositioning. Boarding and entering still require your own Confirm. Native collision and landing restrictions remain unchanged.

Inside the Dimensional Vortex, including its shuffled Geno Dome rooms, the primary Story Events selection follows the local vortex objective without a duplicate route row. Unavailable progress or a missing local control gives a readable status without falling back to the general Lavos route. Distinct optional entries remain available in their categories. World maps, the End of Time and the original Geno Dome keep their existing main objective.

Close Chrono Trigger before installing. Extract the supplied standalone ZIP beside Chrono Trigger.exe and run Install Foresight.cmd. Saves, settings, narration and unrelated mods are preserved. This package does not include the crash collector.

Please retest browsing several categories without reopening the menu, guidance and auto-walk to the parked Epoch, entering the vortex after landing, and Story Events within its entrance and shifting rooms. Send the complete Reloaded gameplay log if any of these stalls or chooses the wrong passage.

Offline tests cover multiple native ticks between controller polls, slow capture and speech, real controller gaps, menu closure feedback, native boarding-table extents, explicit synthetic terrain and larger stress shapes, landing clearance, nonaligned phases, both guidance modes and missing/unreadable local progress. A shared offline workload took about 29 ms and 64–66 MB allocated per update on 0.3.77 versus under 1 ms and under 1 MB after the repair on the same PC. These are synthetic comparison measurements, not the tester's PC or live gameplay. The tester's log does not record why each old menu reset happened; new diagnostics support checking any remaining cause. Live gameplay, physical controller, audible speech and recipient-PC installation tests remain pending.
