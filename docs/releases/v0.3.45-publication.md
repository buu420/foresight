## Tester scope

The [release notes](v0.3.45.md) request End of Time guidance and walking from the starting pillar platform, entry into Spekkio's room, lesson completion/restart, native pillar destination prompts, both parked bikes, and opening/changing/closing the classic party screen. **No live End of Time movement, speech listening, or controller-hook test was performed during publication.** Touch-interface Party remains unsupported. Native Confirm, combat, party changes, race steering, and boosts remain under player control.

The earlier tester reported clearing the Factory and completing the bike race on 0.3.44. That report is distinct from validation performed for this release. Please report missing speech or failed routes with the latest Chrono Trigger text log.
