# Foresight 0.3.79 tester build

Dimensional Vortex navigation now searches the real standing room in the narrow doors of Curious Village, the wall passage and the copied Arris Dome room. The wall's native opening contact can be selected or used as an intermediate route, and navigation stops when its native event takes control. The next cave passage combines its two equivalent contacts into one destination. Story Events follows the next visible child in the quiz room, without supplying an answer. The copied Arris Dome room identifies its door console. Unused destination-zero records and invisible ambush checks no longer appear as destinations.

Sunken Desert exit routes now compensate for moving sand in both manual guidance and auto-walk. Steering rejects movement candidates blocked by the current captured terrain. Exit arrivals retain ordinary exit behavior; chest reach, facing and player Confirm remain separate.

The nine brief flashbacks during Lavos's changing attack modes announce the earlier boss currently shown. Names come from the game's loaded localized text, with the current character name for Magus. These captions do not change battle target names or supply tactics.

Lucca's past now reads the displayed two-row letter panel and its highlighted letter, row and column. It reads the actual glyphs for the game's language. Accepted-letter hide/show gaps stay quiet; a panel closure is announced when the native control returns. Success and timeout continue through the game's own scenes and dialogue. No expected letter, entered-letter history or answer is read. The machine's Story Events entry appears during the accident and no longer gives away the password.
