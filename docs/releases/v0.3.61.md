# Foresight 0.3.61 beta

Enhasa and Kajar books now have stable Book A, B and C labels and announce their
native open or closed state. Manual guidance and automatic walking reach their
normal Confirm positions, including Enhasa's book drawn into the map scenery.
Opening and closing the books describes the visible flame, water or swirling
wisps in the creator's approved narration voice. A passage is described when it
opens. The game still owns the puzzle and every interaction.

Skyway and Land Bridge teleport pads are named under **Interactable Objects**.
Their room doorways remain in **Exits**. A disabled Skyway pad reports that
teleportation is unavailable. End of Time pillars also appear in Interactable
Objects and keep their fixed individual numbers; usable Time Gate objects use
the same Time Gate label. Ordinary room and overworld entrances remain Exits.

The unnamed Skyway room now announces Skyway. The small white dolls in the
audited Enhasa and Kajar rooms have appearance labels. Concealed bookcase
passages stay out of navigation until the native opening completes, and then
appear as Opening behind the bookcase.

The update adds seven embedded narrator recordings. The existing 149 recordings,
opening movie voice, other movie narration, saves and settings are preserved.
Players need no Fish account or network connection for playback.

Installed native scripts, a fresh read-only Ghidra review, rendered sprite
assemblies, reference video and automated regressions support these changes.
The regressions cover both navigation modes, repeated book toggles, duplicate
effect sprites, unavailable transports, unreadable state, concealed passages and
the voice pack. This is not a live gameplay test. Live controller, traversal and
screen-reader listening tests are still needed. See the
[native evidence](../native-audits/2026-10-14-zeal-books-and-transports.md).

Close the game before updating. Use `Foresight-v0.3.61-beta-win-x86.zip` for
standalone installation or `Foresight-v0.3.61-beta-amm.zip` through Accessibility
Mod Manager.
