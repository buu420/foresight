# Foresight 0.3.68 beta

Testing candidate for the Death Peak, Ocean Palace and Blackbird reports.

At Death Peak's windy entrance, Object targets identify shelter below the doll
and the lower and upper trees. Both navigation modes prefer native push-free
floor when available; other approaches stop below the trunk and require the
close horizontal alignment needed for cover. The visible wind-change phase,
strong gust and gust ending are spoken separately from the constant downhill
push and running advice. Native gust knockback regions stay out of Exits.

Automatic walking stops at shelter. On pushed floor, the first arrival words
are **Hold Up now**; the push continues between gusts. The player maintains
cover and chooses when to leave. The doll's calm patch needs no held movement.
Unchanged wind is not repeated after opening the navigation menu or a stop.

Mune appears in People when his head is visible at the Ocean Palace entrance,
even while his feet are below the camera window. The same correction applies to
Masa's matching sprite. Golems concealed beneath the walkway background remain
unlisted until raised. The walkway objective no longer reveals the coming boss.

The reported Dalton snapshots show him above the camera. His name remains gated
by visibility. The supplied gameplay logs reached all expected existing Palace
description cues; the shorter report ended before the later scenes. Existing
approved narration recordings are retained.

Blackbird viewing grates no longer block routes through the shaft's junctions.
Ten actual openings have distinct destination labels; 28 usable viewing grates
are Interactable Objects with stable numbers. Both navigation modes follow the
native duct floor. A grate view announces its current room and return control;
K repeats it without consuming the game's Confirm. Normal crawling footsteps
were already present in the supplied log and remain tied to actual movement.

The controller's R3 navigation menu does not run during a grate view; use K to
repeat and the game's Confirm to return. Room names are not included in grate
labels before inspection. Existing walls, locks, battles and player Confirm
remain native.

Native resource, Ghidra, captured-state and regression evidence is described in
the [navigation audit](../native-audits/2026-10-20-peak-palace-blackbird.md).
The combined regression suites pass 4,319 tests. One unrelated startup-hook
timeout passed on an individual rerun and on the full Mod-suite rerun. The
Release build, standalone package and local deployment are verified separately;
those checks do not start a gameplay session.

No live game, physical controller or audible screen-reader test has been
performed for this candidate. Tester traversal remains necessary.
