# Foresight 0.3.48 beta — battle and party feedback

- Closing a menu cancels its remaining queued speech, including stat lists.
- Selecting an ally in battle reads their visible HP and MP. Party-slot inspection
  does the same. Item-target and Tech character cards keep their displayed stats.
- Knocked-out characters are described as **knocked out** instead of HP zero.
- Holding the native **Dash** control keeps field and world auto-walk active.
  Direction and other action inputs still stop walking; flight controls retain
  their existing cancellation behavior.
- The party-swap reserve roster reads each character's saved name, including
  renamed characters, and identifies the member being moved.
- An unchanged combo list no longer repeats whenever party focus changes. New
  party and swap-preview combo information remains readable when it changes.

AMM Author CLI 0.30.0 was already installed by the other chat. This release uses
that version with its included documentation; no reinstall is needed.

Close the game before updating. Live testing is still needed: close menus during
speech, choose allies with items or Techs, check knockout announcements, hold
Dash during auto-walk, and browse/move reserve members in the party menu. Please
include the latest Chrono Trigger text log if speech or controls fail.
