# Foresight 0.3.43 beta — Site 32 bike race

Adds a reader for Johnny's bike race, which previously went silent after field navigation stopped. Acceleration remains automatic; use the game's normal Up/Down controls to steer and the configured Dash action to boost.

- Brief high, low, and middle tones indicate Johnny above, below, or aligned with your lane on the track overview.
- Speech reports the start, stable lead changes, boosts and recharge, finish-distance milestones, pause, and the native result.
- K or right-stick click repeats current race status. Normal steering, boost, and pause inputs pass through unchanged.
- Cues stop on pause, focus loss, and race exit. Unreadable race state is announced and retried; bounded diagnostics go into the usual log.
- Corrects the Site 32 story hint, which previously promised an on-foot crossing even when the route entered the race trigger.

This is a beta test build. Automated checks cover state capture, input preservation, repeat controls, speech scheduling, tone cancellation, and surrounding readers. A tester still needs to verify a full race and return to exploration. No other minigame coverage is implied.
