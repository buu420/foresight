## Verification

All **2,598 managed tests** passed for the release contents: Native 888, Core 195,
Prism 8, Mod 1,507. These include the supported executable identity and 176
native hook byte contracts. Four release-package tests, six AMM manifest tests
and eighteen release-script tests passed. The fresh build after the source
commit had zero warnings or errors. The packaged DLL identifies that source
commit. Independent read-only review found no important or critical issues;
68 focused Spekkio, End of Time, walking-lesson and castle checks passed.

Fresh Ghidra outputs and installed Atel0284/MapTable0049 prove the return-door
facing and floor-copy behavior. Claude Teammate audited the regular castle doors
in Atel0240/Atel0000 with installed map66 and both approach directions. Its
mixed-effect terrain-trigger finding led to the restricted continuation rule:
all touch actions must be Terrain, apart from audited End of Time exceptions.
Scene268/actor9 therefore retains its existing wait and no preview landmark goals.

The standalone passed all payload hashes, supported-game installer verification,
private x86 runtime launch and altered-payload rejection. The repaired AMM archive
passed identity and content validation with 277 files. Its 275 wrapped standalone
files match byte-for-byte, as do all 28 mod-package files. The manager script remains
staging-only. The final post-repair hash is used in the catalog.

All three uploaded asset sizes and digests matched before publication. All three
public assets were downloaded without authentication and matched their local
hashes. The ordinary public catalog URL was also read without authentication
and matched the entire committed index. Assets were published and verified before
the catalog release record. Catalog validation has zero trust errors, unobtainable
releases, authoring problems and publication blockers.
