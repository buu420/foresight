# Foresight 0.3.64 beta

Entering Frog's hideaway now offers **Left side of the room** in Interactable
Objects while its native reveal is available. Manual guidance and auto-walk
lead into that area, and **Talk to Frog in his hideaway** in Story Events uses
the same approach. Walking across the room's threshold lets the game begin
the scene; no repeated Confirm presses are needed.

Once Frog appears, the room approach is removed. His People entry and the story
objective then use his current position and ordinary native interaction. The
mod does not list a concealed Frog as a visible person.

Offline regressions cover entry, both navigation commands, crossing the strict
floor threshold, stopping when the game takes control, the revealed People
entry, and inactive or unknown native states. The terrain fixture comes from
the installed game's initial map, rather than a captured live map.

All earlier fixes and 160 voice recordings are retained. Saves, settings and
original game files are preserved. Live hideaway traversal, physical controller
behavior and screen-reader listening still need tester confirmation.

See [the native audit](../native-audits/2026-10-16-frog-hideaway.md).

Close the game before updating. Use `Foresight-v0.3.64-beta-win-x86.zip` for
standalone installation or `Foresight-v0.3.64-beta-amm.zip` through Accessibility
Mod Manager.
