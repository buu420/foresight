# Foresight 0.3.44 beta — Factory navigation and Site 32 bike race

Factory conveyors now announce their movement direction and when you leave them. K repeats the current belt status. Conveyor robots have readable positions and hazard labels, and cannot become guidance or automatic-walking destinations. Story Events provides passage objectives from the west inspection drop to the upper walkway, and through the room before the crane to its isolated controls.

Automatic walking uses the native Dash action where opposing the lower belt would otherwise cancel movement. It respects the game's run setting and stops with instructions if a manual run toggle is needed. Perpendicular and with-belt movement do not themselves require running.

The laboratory hatch objective now guides to the entrance fight while its terminal is hidden, then switches to the revealed terminal and the opened ladder. You still control combat and press Confirm yourself.

This release also includes the Site 32 bike-race reader developed in 0.3.43, which was not published separately. Brief high, low, and middle tones indicate Johnny above, below, or aligned with your lane. Speech reports the start, lead changes, boosts and recharge, remaining distance, pause, and result. K or right-stick click (R3) repeats race status. Acceleration is automatic; you steer with the game's normal Up/Down controls and boost with your configured Dash action.

**This is a beta for testing.** Automated checks passed, but a complete live Factory route and bike race remain unverified. Please test conveyor speech and K repeat, escaping the west inspection drop, entering and returning from the crane alcove, opening the laboratory hatch, and a full race followed by working exploration. At the west inspection drop, choose **Story Events → Reach the warehouse walkways**. Start guidance or automatic walking again after an area change. If a reader goes silent or a route fails, [report the problem](https://github.com/buu420/foresight/issues) with the latest Chrono Trigger text log from `%APPDATA%\Reloaded-Mod-Loader-II\Logs`.
